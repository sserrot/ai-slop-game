Conditions

In vacuum the body rolls a half turn about its travel axis and hauls along hanging under the rail — a zero-G module's rails are its floor-and-ceiling pair, and the server threads the creature onto one. Toggle it while walking to watch it turn over.

Motion

Toggle that to see what this pass did. Sine only is the previous creature: every joint driven by one phase scalar, so the head sweeps like a wiper, the tail arrives at the same instant the hips do and nothing on the body is ever still.

Secondary motion adds the three things a sine wave cannot do — lag (sprung tail and head, underdamped, so a turn throws the tail wide and it settles late), hold and snap (the head dwells dead still, then whips to a new bearing in about a tenth of a second, and the gait freezes with it) and breath (a rib cycle at its own rate, beating against the gait). Zero extra triangles.

Watch it on SEARCH, parked for the hold, and on HUNT, walking for the tail. The shadow on the back wall is the other half of this pass: the alien now casts into §9's one shadow map.

Surface

Flesh shader gives the creature its own program: subsurface wrap so light bleeds past the terminator and comes back reddened, a grazing rim, procedural skin detail with no texture and no UVs, and a millimetre-scale crawl. It changes how the surface answers light and adds none — so with the torch off the alien is still invisible, which is a rule and not a nicety.
