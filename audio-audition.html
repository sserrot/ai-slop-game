02De-alarming pass

The offenders that read as smoke detector: piercing mid-band tones with hard attacks. The proposals keep every gameplay job (countdown rhythm, urgency axis, audibility) but move the information into pitch-register, pulse and pressure instead of shriek.

03The alien, rebuilt

It looks like an alligator, so it should sound like one: a pulse-train larynx (a 25–45 Hz glottal buzz pushed through throat formant filters) instead of detuned synth saws, wet crackle in the breath, and vocabulary borrowed from real gators — the bellow with its infrasonic “water-dance” tremor, the open-mouth hiss, the chuff. Same growl core everywhere, so every call is recognisably one animal.

04Spooky ambience

The current bed is honest machinery — fans, mains, a pump. The proposal keeps it and adds a dread layer underneath: a beating sub-drone, a barely-tonal detuned pad, and scheduled far-off events (hull groans, stress pings, distant thumps, skitters) that keep the station feeling inhabited by something. Quiet on purpose — it must never mask a footstep.
